Ono Saika

無機質な静寂と、触れ得ない有機の体温。
その境界線に、生きている人間の気配を宿したい。

装飾的で研ぎ澄まされた無機質な空間。その静謐な秩序のなかに、たしかに体温を持ち呼吸する、生身の人間の気配を閉じ込めること。それが私の絵画の原点です。

心から惹かれる対象と向き合うとき、衝動に身を任せず、理性で自らにブレーキをかける――その判断には切実な痛みが伴います。「理性（無機）」と「情動（有機）」のあいだに生まれる静かな緊張感こそが、瞳の深みや肌の透明感を引き立てる最高の絵の具となります。同性という近しい存在だからこそ抱く、憧憬と敬畏の念。触れ得ない一線を画すことで、対象の美しさは永遠の気品を宿します。

その気品を確かな絵肌として結実させるため、見えない細部まで職人技を尽くします。ゼロから価値を創り出す人々への敬意を胸に、無機質な空間に本物の生命感をもたらす一枚を創り出しています。

Inorganic silence, and the organic warmth that can never be touched.
On that boundary, I want the presence of a living human being to dwell.

A decorative, finely honed inorganic space. Within its serene order, to enclose the presence of a living, breathing human being who truly carries body warmth — that is the origin of my painting.

When I face a subject that truly draws me in, I do not surrender to impulse; I hold myself back with reason — a decision that carries a keen, genuine pain. The quiet tension that arises between "reason (the inorganic)" and "emotion (the organic)" becomes the finest paint, bringing out the depth of the eyes and the translucence of the skin. The yearning and reverence I feel precisely because the subject is someone so close, of the same sex as myself. By drawing a line that cannot be crossed, the beauty of the subject comes to hold an eternal grace.

To bring that grace to fruition as a convincing painted surface, I devote my craft to every detail unseen. With respect for those who create value from nothing, I continue to create paintings that bring genuine vitality into the inorganic space.
